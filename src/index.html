Before you jump in

What is Unpaused?

Unpaused (unpaused.online) is a free browser game site. It is home to three original games, Aurora, Worldloom and TacticStrike, plus the open-source browser classics HexGL, 2048 and Hextris, credited to their creators. You open a game from the home page and play it in your browser without installing a game launcher.

Are the games free to play?

Yes. You can play Aurora, Worldloom and TacticStrike for free in your browser. TacticStrike also has an optional credit shop.

Do I need to download anything?

No game download or launcher is required. Open a game from this page. Use an up-to-date desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse; Worldloom also needs WebGL 2 and hardware acceleration.

What kind of game is Aurora?

Aurora is a 3D space maze, only on Unpaused. You guide a small astronaut across floating crystal islands and, in Chapter 2, a living jungle island, collecting three star shards to wake the portal while dodging lasers, drones and insects. It has full controller support.

Can I play TacticStrike alone?

Yes. TacticStrike is a top-down tactical shooter with online battles, and you can practise against bots before your next match.

Can I play Worldloom with a friend?

Yes. Create an account, share your four-digit friend code, and invite one player when creating a saved world. Either player can join that shared world while the other is offline.

What does an account give me?

Your account holds your username, friend code, Worldloom invites, and up to ten saved worlds. You can manage or delete your worlds from the account panel.
